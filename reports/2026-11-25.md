**Abstract**
We give four large language models (LLMs) of the Claude family the complete weights of a 20k-parameter CIFAR-10 CNN as text and ask them to produce a 4-bit network without tools, data or the ability to run inference. Standard round-to-nearest (RTN) quantization to 4 bit lowers the test accuracy from 81.4% to 60.4%. **Abstract**
Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly used to translate natural-language statements into first-order logic (FOL) to improve reasoning. However, LLMs are prone to errors, and so a semantically unfaithful FOL formula could corrupt downstream reasoning. We present FOL-Proof, a semantic firewall pipeline that validates LLM-generated FOL translations against independently derived linguistic interpretations.
